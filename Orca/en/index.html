ORCA Marine Trips (أوركا للرحلات البحرية) is a Saudi booking service for yacht and boat rentals in Jeddah on the Red Sea, with 26 boats and yachts departing from Al-Amanah Yacht Club in South Obhur, Red Sea Marina, Al-Ahlam Marina, Al-Lulua (Pearl) Marina and the south Jeddah marinas. A 6-hour Bayada Island trip starts at 1,300 SAR for up to 9 guests, Obhur creek cruises start at 350 SAR per hour, and fishing trips start at 1,200 SAR for 8 hours. Bookings are confirmed on WhatsApp (+966 56 839 0147) with a deposit; every passenger needs a national ID or iqama for the Border Guard sailing permit.

Deposit Policy: Deposit is only paid after confirming your booking and departure time with the captain — the remaining balance is paid upon boarding on trip day.

Coast Guard Regulations & Policies

Terms & Booking Guidelines

We strictly comply with official Coast Guard safety laws to ensure peace of mind on every voyage across the Red Sea.

Simple Booking Steps

  • 1. Choose Package: Select your preferred vessel and cruise date.
  • 2. Pay Deposit: Pay the deposit to confirm booking with the captain; remaining balance paid upon boarding on trip day.
  • 3. Present Original ID: Present original physical ID or via official apps (Tawakkalna / Absher).
🚫 Digital photos or copies are strictly not accepted — physical original or official app only.

Sailing Permit Requirements

The following details are required for each passenger to issue the official Coast Guard sailing permit:

  • National ID or Iqama Number
  • Full Date of Birth

Important Security Notices

  • • Ensure your national ID / Iqama is active and valid.
  • • Biometric fingerprint verification is conducted at the marina gate.
  • • Strict compliance with captain and Coast Guard directives is mandatory.
🚫 In case of regulation violation or late arrival, trips are cancelled without refund.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions about Boat Trips in Jeddah

How much is a Bayada Island trip in Jeddah?

A 6-hour Bayada Island trip starts at 1,300 SAR on the Seven, Shaheen, Bahr and Qimat Al-Fawz boats (up to 9 guests), from 1,880 SAR on baby yachts such as Al-Jawhari, Tam, Atlas Wa Bas and Jood 007 (up to 11 guests), 3,680 SAR on Nardo, 6,980 SAR on Moorie VIP (10 guests), and 30,000 SAR on the Aqua Luxury Yacht (up to 25 guests). Abu Tair Island is usually +300 SAR, and some boats add 200 SAR at weekends.

How much is an Obhur creek cruise?

Creek cruises in Sharm Obhur start at 350 SAR per hour (200 SAR for half an hour) on standard boats, 480 SAR/hr on Al-Jawhari, Atlas Wa Bas, Jood 007 and the Al-Jawhari Speedboat, 580 SAR/hr on Barbaros and Tam, 680 SAR/hr on Nardo, 980 SAR/hr on Moorie, 2,000 SAR/hr on the Royal Luxury Yacht, and 5,500 SAR/hr on the Aqua yacht (minimum 3 hours, up to 70 guests).

Which marina do the trips depart from?

ORCA trips depart from Jeddah marinas: Al-Amanah Yacht Club in South Obhur (Barbaros, Moorie, Nardo, Tam, Al-Jawhari, Atlas Wa Bas, Jood 007, Al-Jawhari Speedboat), Red Sea Marina, Marsa Al Ahlam, Al-Lulua (Pearl) Marina (Aqua yacht), and the southern Jeddah marinas for fishing boats.

How do I book a boat trip with ORCA?

Choose a boat from the fleet, check the cost in the price calculator, then send your booking on WhatsApp at +966 56 839 0147 or call +966 53 140 3454. A deposit confirms the booking and the balance is paid on boarding.

What do I need on the day of the trip?

Each guest needs a national ID or Iqama number and full date of birth for the Border Guard sailing permit, and must show the original ID or the Tawakkalna / Absher app (photos are not accepted). Guests are verified by fingerprint at the marina.

How many guests can a boat or yacht take?

Fishing and standard boats take 8 to 9 guests, most baby yachts 11, Nardo and Norseen 14, Moorie 15, the Royal Luxury Yacht 30, and the Aqua yacht up to 70 guests on creek cruises.

Are there fishing trips in Jeddah?

Yes. The southern boats (Jaguar, Ghazal Obhur, Bin Shuraiq, Shawq Al-Layl) run fishing trips from 1,200 SAR for 8 hours to 1,500 SAR for 12 hours, Al-Ameed offers a 12-hour fishing trip for 1,800 SAR, and Baby Yacht Ambassador offers 6-hour trolling for 2,200 SAR.

Are there shared trips with individual seats?

Yes, shared Bayada trips with individual seats: Economy 250 SAR, VIP 300 SAR and VVIP 320 SAR per person.
