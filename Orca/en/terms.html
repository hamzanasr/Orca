Official Regulations

Terms & Conditions of Booking & Sailing

We strictly adhere to official Saudi Coast Guard safety laws to guarantee maximum safety and enjoyment in the Red Sea.

1. Simple Booking Steps

Select Vessel & Date

Choose your preferred yacht or boat and specify departure time.

Pay 50% Deposit

Deposit confirms your reservation and locks the boat slot exclusively for you.

Present Original IDs

Present physical National ID/Iqama or digital via (Tawakkalna/Absher) at the marina.

2. Coast Guard Sailing Permit Data

The following details are required for every passenger to issue official Coast Guard sailing permits:

  • Full National ID or Iqama Number for each passenger.
  • Full Date of Birth (Day / Month / Year).
  • Passport Details & Visa copy for international tourists.

3. Essential Security & Safety Rules

  • Physical ID or Official App Required: Photocopies or camera photos of IDs are strictly prohibited by Coast Guard authorities.
  • Fingerprint Verification: All passengers undergo identity biometric verification at the marina gate.
  • Arrival Time: Please arrive at the marina 20 minutes prior to scheduled departure.
  • Weather & Coast Guard Decisions: If sailing is halted by Coast Guard due to weather, your trip can be rescheduled or refunded.
