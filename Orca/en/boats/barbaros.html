Barbaros VIP Boat (Barbarossa)

A luxury double-decker pontoon boat for up to 11 guests (VIP Package strictly up to 10 guests), offering total family privacy in Bayadah and Abu Tair islands, outdoor cinema projector for celebrations, on-board slush machine, and a full floating water park with slide.

Specifications & Amenities

  • Cruise Crew: Filipino captain for Regular Package; Filipino captain and Filipino female assistant for VIP Package for maximum service & family privacy.
  • Outdoor Cinema Projector: Night cinema showing Netflix, Disney movies, and football matches after sunset — FREE for celebration & party packages exceeding 2 hours with complimentary popcorn and soft drinks.
  • Exclusive Water Toys: Built-in water slide & stairs, floating sunbed platform, crane hammock chair, sea kayak, large games (floating tent, water volleyball, circular lounge), and towable tube rides (subject to wave & weather conditions).
  • Free Guest Souvenirs (VIP Package): Beach tote bag, waterproof phone pouch, water shoes, ear/nose plugs (towels and snorkeling goggles are provided for on-board use).
  • Slush Machine & Hospitality: On-board slush machine for icy fruit drinks on all cruises + complimentary American coffee & tea (red or green) for picnic cruises and dinner trips.
  • Facilities: Marine restroom and freshwater shower (no enclosed air-conditioned indoor salon or bedroom).
  • Departure Marina: Al-Amanah Yacht Club (South Obhur / Sharm Obhur, Jeddah).
