Barbaros VIP Boat (Barbarossa)

✨ An extraordinary marine journey combining luxury, private crew service, water sports, and open-air cinema!

Barbaros VIP Boat is one of Jeddah's finest luxury private boats stationed at Al-Amanah Yacht Club (South Obhur). Designed with high privacy for families and private groups, it features a dedicated Filipino crew (man & woman), inflatable water park toys, night cinema projector, barbecue grill, and customized catering.

Specifications & Amenities

  • Dedicated Filipino Crew (Male & Female): Seamless five-star hospitality with utmost privacy for families.
  • Outdoor Cinema Projector: Night cinema showing Netflix, Disney movies, and football matches with free popcorn and soft drinks.
  • Exclusive Water Toys: Giant water slide, sunbeds, hammock chair, kayak, floating water tent, water volleyball, and floating trampoline.
  • Free Guest Gifts: Beach tote bag, waterproof phone pouch, water shoes, ear/nose plugs, plus complimentary beach towels and snorkeling goggles.
  • Complimentary Hospitality: Fresh American coffee upon departure + cold water and sodas throughout the cruise.
  • Full Indoor Comfort: Air-conditioned lounge salon, master cabin, modern bathroom, and freshwater shower.
  • Departure Marina: Al-Amanah Yacht Club (South Obhur / Sharm Obhur, Jeddah).
