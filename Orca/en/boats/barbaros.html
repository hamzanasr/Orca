Barbaros VIP Boat (Barbarossa)

A luxury double-decker pontoon boat featuring full family privacy, open-air seating, water activities, an outdoor cinema projector, and hospitality bar in Sharm Obhur.

Specifications & Amenities

  • Cruise Crew: 1 professional captain for standard cruises (6 & 9 hours), with a dedicated captain and female assistant on 6-hour VIP cruises for superior privacy.
  • Outdoor Cinema Projector: Night cinema showing Netflix, Disney movies, and football matches after sunset with complimentary popcorn and soft drinks.
  • Exclusive Water Toys: Built-in water slide, floating sunbed platform, crane hammock chair, floating tent, water volleyball, circular water lounge, and towable tube.
  • Free Guest Souvenirs: Beach tote bag, waterproof phone pouch, water shoes, ear/nose plugs (towels and snorkeling goggles are provided for on-board use).
  • Slush Machine & Hospitality: On-board slush machine for icy fruit drinks on all cruises + complimentary American coffee & tea for picnic cruises over 1 hour and dinner cruises.
  • Facilities: Marine restroom and freshwater shower (no enclosed air-conditioned indoor salon or bedroom).
  • Departure Marina: Al-Amanah Yacht Club (South Obhur / Sharm Obhur, Jeddah).

Barbarossa VIP Boat Packages & Rates

Barbarossa VIP Boat cruise options are divided into two primary categories:

Part 1: Open Sea Cruises (Bayadah or Abu Tair)

Abu Tair Only: Enhanced Family Privacy
Open Sea Timings & Policies:
  • Destinations: Bayadah Island, or Abu Tair Island (higher privacy for families).
  • 6-Hour Cruise Timings (2 trips daily): Slot 1: (5:00 or 6:00 AM) to (11:00 AM or 12:00 PM) | Slot 2: 1:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
  • 9-Hour Cruise Timing (1 trip daily): (5:00 or 6:00 AM) to (2:00 or 3:00 PM).
  • Trip Confirmation: Final confirmation takes place 24 hours prior to verify weather & maritime conditions.
  • Coast Guard Sailing Permit: A copy of National ID or Iqama must be sent after final trip confirmation to issue the official permit.
  • Max Capacity: Up to 10 guests.
  • Crew on Board: 1 Captain only for 6-hour & 9-hour Standard Packages; Captain with Female Assistant for the 6-hour VIP Package.
