Al-Jawhari Yacht (يخت الجوهري)

A luxury air-conditioned baby yacht accommodating up to 10 guests for Bayada Island, Abu Tair, Khor Saud, and creek tours in Jeddah.

Specifications & Features

  • Forward Sunlounger Deck: Cushioned front deck for relaxing and sunbathing
  • Air-Conditioned Interior: Cozy private cabin with a salon, rest bedroom, and preparation kitchenette with sink and storage
  • Private Restroom & Fresh Shower: Fully equipped onboard bathroom with freshwater rinse shower
  • Shaded Teak Decking: Premium teak floors with comfortable cushioned seating and full shade sun canopy with safety netting
  • Twin Engines & Swim Platform: Powerful smooth cruising with rear swim ladder for easy, safe swimming access
  • Water Sports & Toys: Stand-up paddleboard (SUP), floating water mattress, plus optional towable Banana Boat game
  • Capacity & Privacy: Accommodates up to 10 guests with ultimate privacy for families and VIP groups
